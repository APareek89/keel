1. MEDDIC before anything is forecast. Missing economic buyer is a stage cap, not a note.
2. Champion tested, not assumed — will they introduce you to the buyer? If not, they are a coach.
3. Security and legal started at 50%, never at verbal yes. Kotak is the standing example.
4. Multi-year structure offered before discount. Term is cheaper than price.
5. Mutual close plan, shared with the customer in writing.
6. Anything past 25% discount goes to Sanjay first.
