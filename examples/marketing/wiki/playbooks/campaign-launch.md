1. Message agreed with brand before any creative is commissioned.
2. Forecast blended CAC impact and check against the ceiling — this is the gate that actually stops things.
3. Geo-holdout designed before launch, not after. Retrofitting a holdout is impossible.
4. Daily pacing caps set with the agency; alert at ±8%, escalate to Vikram at ±12%.
5. Landing pages live and tracking-verified 72h before spend.
6. Post-campaign read at day 14 and day 45. The day-14 read is directional only — say so, or it gets quoted as final.
